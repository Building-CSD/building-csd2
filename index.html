What happens here

Every event and program is built around one or more of these four practices.

Pause Taking 20 minutes to reset, every day. Reflect Working out what they think and feel. Dinner dialogue with Presti Talk Discussing how to live with other people. Live Applying your learnings and insights to a real choice about their life.
